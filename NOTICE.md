**Title screen:** the cover image was supplied by the project creator and generated with AI assistance.

**Engine captures:** selected images are rendered in Unreal Engine. The mannequins are temporary. Third-party models visible in the environment are credited with their authors, sources and licences in [docs/ASSET_CREDITS.md](docs/ASSET_CREDITS.md).

**Original comparison references:** when present, they are labelled as original-game references. Enlarging a low-resolution reference does not add new source detail. Comparison plates and alpha captures are labelled separately.

**Scope:** this repository is documentation and selected presentation media. It makes no blanket licence grant over the artwork, logos, original references or depicted third-party assets. Downloadable assets, if ever offered, need their own explicit terms.
