## Widescreen presentation

4:3 remains the basis for matched original comparisons. 16:9 captures are a separate presentation of the wider reconstructed scene. They must preserve proportions and explain any differences in visible framing.

[![16:9 capture with the 4:3 reference area outlined](../media/wide/mist-view15-guide.png)](../media/wide/mist-view15-16x9.png)

The 16:9 image keeps the vertical field of view of the 4:3 reference: the centred 4:3 area (2880 px) matches the reference framing and the 480 px on each side show extra rebuilt space. The [native 16:9 capture](../media/wide/mist-view15-16x9.png) is untouched; the guide outlines the 4:3 area.
